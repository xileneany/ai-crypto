01
BTC + ETF Jun 1–6
Bitcoin hits $59,100 — 2026 low — as ETF outflows reach 13 consecutive days and $4.4B total
The most severe institutional selling streak in the ETF era. From May 15 to June 3, US spot Bitcoin ETFs bled $4.37B across 13 consecutive sessions — the longest streak since the products launched in January 2024. BlackRock's IBIT absorbed $3.3B of the total damage. Total ETF AUM collapsed from $104.3B to $82.8B in three weeks — a 22% decline. Bitcoin tracked every dollar of the outflows: BTC fell from ~$79K to a 2026 low of $59,100 on June 5 before recovering to ~$61,700. Drivers: 13 consecutive days of Iran re-escalation, persistent inflation, capital rotating to AI stocks and gold, and — crucially — Strategy's first Bitcoin sale in nearly four years (a small dividend-related disposal that was symbolically devastating for sentiment). Long-term holders now control 5.3M BTC at a loss — the largest amount of underwater long-term supply since the COVID crash. But on June 4, the streak broke: $3M in net ETF inflows ended the run. Then June 5's hot jobs report triggered another $1.7B in liquidations before the weekend recovery.
02
ETH Jun 1–7
Ethereum falls 21% on the week — 17 consecutive ETF outflow days, $1,900 support broken, Polymarket prices $1,500 risk
Ethereum suffered its worst week of 2026. ETH fell from ~$2,040 to $1,615 by Saturday June 7 — a 21% weekly drop — while breaking below the critical $1,900 support that analysts had identified as the line between "bullish structure" and "medium-term breakdown." ETH ETFs posted 17 consecutive outflow days — their longest streak on record — with cumulative net inflows shrinking to just $11.24B. In 24 hours on June 3 alone, $475M in ETH long positions were liquidated. Solana open interest surged to a record even as SOL prices fell — a technical signal that aggressive short accumulation is underway. The $60,000 BTC strike put on Deribit carries over $1B in notional open interest, signaling institutional hedging against a further leg lower. Standard Chartered head of digital assets Geoff Kendrick told clients BTC's buying zone is $62,000–$64,000 and the "bottom is almost in." Polymarket separately priced an ETH dip to $1,500 at elevated probability.
03
XRP Jun 1–7
XRP outperforms — $131M in May ETF inflows, second straight week of positive ETF flows while BTC/ETH bled $2B
XRP was the clearest relative winner of the week. While BTC and ETH bled billions, XRP ETFs absorbed $131M in net inflows in May — the second consecutive week of positive XRP ETF flows despite the broader outflow storm. In the week of May 20–29, XRP ETFs attracted $35M while BTC and ETH lost $2B combined. By June 7, XRP traded at ~$1.10 — down 15% on the week but significantly outperforming ETH (–21%) and BTC (–14%). Technical picture: XRP remains above its 200-day MA at $1.1230; the MACD is positive; RSI at 74.01 signals overbought but sustained. Brad Garlinghouse praised SEC Chair Atkins as a "breath of fresh air," and Ripple stated it is "closer than ever to CLARITY Act passage." If the CLARITY Act passes, XRP and the XRPL ecosystem benefit most directly from commodity/payment-stablecoin classification. XRP's quantum resistance roadmap (targeting 2028) also continues to attract security-focused institutions.
04
Macro Jun 5
Strongest jobs report in 13 months kills June rate-cut hopes — BNP Paribas now forecasts three rate hikes by year-end
The May nonfarm payrolls report, released June 5, was the second-strongest US jobs print in 13 months. The reaction was immediate: crypto long positions worth $1.7B were liquidated in 24 hours. The Nasdaq fell ~5% — its steepest drop since April 2025. Treasury yields surged again. BNP Paribas abandoned its rate-hold forecast and now projects three Federal Reserve rate hikes beginning in December 2026. Polymarket assigns 52% probability to a rate increase before year-end; CME FedWatch shows 42.7% chance of higher rates by December. Analysts at Presto Research said BTC's slump reflects competition from AI stocks and gold as investors reassess Fed cut prospects. Saylor's Strategy made a small BTC sale to fund dividend obligations — the symbolism was far larger than the amount; "if even Saylor is selling" was the sentiment. Short-term BTC holders are now realizing losses at record levels; the 30-day realized P&L ratio hit an all-time low.
05
Fan tokens · CHZ Jun 1–7
CHZ collapses to $0.028 — Belgium BELG fan token launches June 3, buyback burns 4.66M tokens, World Cup 4 days away
The fan token market suffered a brutal collision between its strongest catalysts and the worst macro environment of the year. On June 1, Chiliz executed a 4.66M CHZ buyback and burn under its Vision 2030 program. On June 2, Chiliz announced the official Belgium national team ($BELG) fan token partnership with the RBFA — 2M tokens at $1.00 each launching June 3. The news moved CHZ +7% in a single session. Then Bitcoin collapsed below $62,000 on June 4, contributing to $623M in BTC long liquidations alone. The macro selling overwhelmed the fan token narrative entirely. CHZ dropped from the $0.044 level to approximately $0.028 — its lowest since early 2026 and well below RSI 21.48 (deeply oversold). Despite this, the World Cup kicks off June 11 in Mexico City — just 4 days from today. PSG won the UCL. Belgium, Argentina, and Portugal fan tokens are live. Chiliz holds MiCA authorization in Europe (450M+ users). The structural case is intact; the macro timing was maximally bad.
Key tension

The worst week of 2026 — and the most important structural signal is the streak ending

$4.4B out in 13 days. Then $3M in. The bleeding stopped.
The numbers are stark: 13 consecutive ETF outflow days. 17 consecutive ETH ETF outflows. $8.7B in liquidations across five trading days. BTC's 2026 low at $59,100. ETH down 21% in a week. Strategy selling BTC for the first time in four years.

And then on June 4, the ETF outflow streak ended. $3M in. The bleeding stopped.

This is the most important single data point of the week. Standard Chartered's Geoff Kendrick called the $62K–$64K zone "the buying zone we all wanted." Glassnode noted that ETF holdings have been stable since February despite the dollar outflows — because BTC price fell, not because coins were actually liquidated. The structural position of 4% of all BTC supply held in ETFs is intact.

The macro headwinds are real: jobs data that kills June cut hopes, BNP Paribas forecasting rate hikes, Iran re-escalation. But the sell-the-rally phase may be over. The question for the week of June 8–14 is whether the June 16–17 FOMC can deliver any relief — and whether the World Cup fan token narrative survives the macro damage to CHZ.
The XRP signal the market missed
While BTC and ETH bled $2B in ETF outflows in the same week, XRP ETFs were net positive for the second consecutive week. This is a relative strength signal that institutional allocators are differentiating within crypto. XRP's regulatory tailwind (commodity classification likely under CLARITY Act), Ripple's XRPL quantum resistance roadmap, and Garlinghouse's "closer than ever" CLARITY Act comments suggest XRP is the first beneficiary when US crypto regulation clarifies. In a market this risk-off, holding the one token with a regulatory catalyst and positive ETF flows is not a coincidence.
